Message from the Chairman

Dr. Rahat Hossain Faisal

Associate Professor & Chairman

"I am truly honored to extend a warm welcome to you all to the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at the University of Barishal. This department is where exceptional minds converge to shape their careers at prestigious organizations both locally and internationally. We take pride in inspiring the brightest individuals to explore and advance the cutting-edge technologies in Computer Science and Engineering.
Our goal is to prepare you for the future, equipping you to address complex societal challenges with the help of emerging technologies that enhance the quality of human life. We view every member of our community as a driver of change, motivating them to move beyond conventional learning and approaches, to create new knowledge and encourage others to do the same. The success of our educational methods is evident in the remarkable achievements of our students, who excel in national and international competitions. Our alumni, who continue to shine in their respective fields, further bolster our reputation. Our students are thoroughly prepared to rise to the occasion and emerge as the leaders of tomorrow. We welcome innovative and motivated individuals who are eager to embrace the challenges of the 21st-century workforce and are passionate about contributing to the development of a smarter Bangladesh."
