Our Programs

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at the University of Barishal offers both undergraduate and postgraduate programs that are designed to equip students with the theoretical knowledge and practical skills needed to excel in the fast-paced and ever-evolving field of computer science.

Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Computer Science and Engineering

Our Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Computer Science and Engineering is a comprehensive program that covers fundamental aspects of computer science, including programming, data structures, algorithms, computer networks, databases, and software engineering. The BSc program emphasizes hands-on learning, problem-solving, and research, providing students with the technical expertise required to succeed in the industry and academia. Graduates are well-prepared to take on roles in software development, IT consulting, cybersecurity, and more.

Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science and Engineering

Our Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science and Engineering program offers advanced study in specialized areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, and computer vision. The MSc program is designed for those who wish to deepen their knowledge, engage in cutting-edge research, and develop expertise in emerging technologies. It provides students with a strong foundation for leadership roles in technology companies, research institutions, and startups. Through a combination of coursework, research, and practical experience, our MSc students are equipped to drive innovation and contribute significantly to the advancement of computer science and engineering.
